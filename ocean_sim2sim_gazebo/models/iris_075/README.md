# iris_075: 3DR Iris scaled 0.75 (drone to grasp)

## Source of the meshes

`meshes/iris.stl`, `meshes/iris_prop_ccw.dae`, `meshes/iris_prop_cw.dae` are copied unchanged from the
3DR Iris model of PX4 Gazebo-classic:

- repository: https://github.com/PX4/PX4-SITL_gazebo-classic, `models/iris/meshes/`,
  commit `70683dc759cbd3ebccfa78429b564f8b1fb5d149` (as a submodule of PX4-Autopilot v1.16)
- authors: Lorenz Meier and Thomas Gubler (`models/iris/model.config`)
- license: the repository declares `BSD` in its `package.xml` but has no LICENSE file. PX4 is licensed under
  BSD 3-Clause; its license text is included here as `LICENSE_PX4` (PX4-Autopilot).
- the Isaac Sim drone (Pegasus Simulator Iris, BSD 3-Clause) is the same mesh (18,171 vertices) turned 180 deg about z.

## Generated files (do not edit)

`model.sdf` and `meshes/collision/hull_*.stl` are generated by `ocean_sim2sim_gazebo/scripts/build_iris_drone.py`
from `ocean_sim2sim_gazebo/config/iris_drone.yaml` (needs `pip install trimesh coacd scipy`):

- scale 0.75, yaw 180 deg (Isaac Sim orientation)
- body collision: CoACD convex decomposition (32 hulls, at most 64 vertices each)
- mass 0.8 kg = body 0.78 (uniform density over the hulls) + 4 rotor discs x 0.005 (fixed)
